# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, in the existing personal-site repository.

## Users

People who love Ted Lasso and want a useful piece of perspective for a specific moment, feeling, or situation.

## Product Purpose

The Lasso Way is a fan-made quote discovery experiment. A visitor searches for a situation, receives a short piece of wisdom from the show, sees the season and episode evidence, and can share the result. A single featured line refreshes on each load.

## Positioning

It maps a real-life situation to a small, evidence-backed moment from Ted Lasso rather than presenting a generic quote collection.

## Operating Context

The route is a focused mini-project with its own visual world, separate from the surrounding personal site. Visitors may arrive, search by mood or situation, or browse the full field.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Search by situation, feeling, or theme.
- Explore the full quote set with search and theme filters.
- Ingest local TXT, SRT, and VTT transcript files into a review queue.
- Rank likely shareable lines, then keep, edit, mark maybe, or skip them.
- Export kept lines as a provenance-first site import bundle for library enrichment.
- Show a short quote, speaker, season, episode, episode title, and source link.
- Show one featured quote with its episode context.
- Support copy and share actions with a stable quote URL.
- Include a visual reference for the quote when an appropriate asset exists.
- Exact show stills require a licensed source or user-supplied assets; the initial experiment should use original editorial illustrations and explicit scene references until that is resolved.
- Preserve the fan-made, unaffiliated disclosure.

## Brand Commitments

The name is The Lasso Way. The experience should carry the warmth, optimism, wit, and emotional clarity associated with the show while avoiding a replica of the surrounding site. The user explicitly requested a believe illustration, show-themed art, and episode-level evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

The current prototype contains a small curated set of Ted Lasso quotes with episode metadata and working search, theme filters, a rotating featured line, copy, share, and transcript review flows. The transcript desk is local-only and starts empty until licensed or user-supplied files are added. No licensed stills or user-supplied image assets are present yet.

## Product Principles

- Meet the visitor in the situation they are actually in.
- Make every quote traceable to an episode.
- Keep the interaction light enough to use in a hard moment.
- Make a result worth sharing without turning it into a content farm.
- Treat rights and attribution as part of the product, not cleanup work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should support keyboard use, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, and text alternatives for all decorative and reference imagery.
